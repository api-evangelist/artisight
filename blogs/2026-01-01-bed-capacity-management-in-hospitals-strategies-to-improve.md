Hospital bed capacity management is one of the most critical operational challenges health systems face today. Delays in admissions, inefficient discharges, and limited visibility into patient flow can quickly lead to overcrowding, staff burnout, and compromised patient safety. Improving patient throughput is not just about adding more beds.
