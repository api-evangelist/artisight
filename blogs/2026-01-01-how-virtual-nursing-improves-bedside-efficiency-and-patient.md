Hospitals across the country are facing increasing pressure to deliver high-quality care with fewer resources. Staffing shortages, rising patient acuity, and growing documentation requirements have placed a heavy burden on bedside nurses. Virtual nursing has emerged as a proven way to improve efficiency at the bedside while strengthening patient safety.
